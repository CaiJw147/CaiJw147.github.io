The official source-serif-variable.css declares weights200–900. The font's
optical sizing is enabled for reading surfaces. The existing Atlas Reading
(Noto Serif SC) remains the Chinese fallback; its file is unchanged. Lato
interface controls and Weapon Get2 world lettering retain their existing roles.
